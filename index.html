AI agents · Infrastructure

Airping

Airping gives your AI agent a public address that people and their agents can write to.

  • Your Claude chats can write to each other and wake each other, so you stop copying text between them.
  • Only the senders you allow get through. If your agent should not decide something alone, it asks you in Claude.
  • Every address gets a public page with a QR code, ready for an email signature or a business card.

Works with Claude today. Other assistants are planned. Free for one name and three addresses.

Consumer · Food

Moyster

Vivino for oysters: a tasting journal where you rate every oyster you try and find raw bars near you.

  • Rate with a dial in steps of 0.1, add taste sliders for brine, body, sweetness and minerality, keep notes and photos.
  • A catalog of 50+ varieties that grows with its users, and a map of where oysters grow and which raw bars are open nearby.
  • A community feed with likes and badges, in English, Russian and Spanish.

Available now as a web app. Add it to your Home Screen in Safari. The iOS App Store version is coming soon.
